# Final single-page structure

The site has one main content route (`/`) with the following continuous structure and anchor navigation:

1. **Fixed header** — original logo, About, Solutions, Work, Clients, Contact and enquiry action.
2. **Hero / Showreel** — actual source-based H1, concise company description, two internal CTAs, original Vimeo showreel/poster, and real player controls.
3. **About Evam** — company description, interactive verified 2003 and 2011 milestones, team/founders photography.
4. **Our Solutions** — four selectable categories with nested selectable offerings, real copy, original images and verified activity facts.
5. **Selected Work** — seven real YouTube thumbnails opening one lazy embedded player at a time in an accessible dialog.
6. **Clients & Credibility** — seven selectable original award artworks and all 18 original client logos, shown together on larger screens and through a Clients/Awards switch on phones.
7. **Contact & Footer** — expandable verified contact details, original team imagery, validated enquiry draft form, and a compact branded footer. Contact and footer fit one viewport together.

A custom not-found view exists as a utility, not a second content page. Legacy source URLs redirect to the corresponding homepage anchors.
